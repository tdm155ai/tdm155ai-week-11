# Hidden rule

One player invents a secret rule; the others discover it by playing.

**Shows it:** *Zendo*

**Try it in two minutes:** One person thinks of a rule about objects on the table. Others place objects and hear only yes or no.

**Build it:** Claude holds the rule; players show cards to `/draw-a-box`, and Claude says yes or no.
