# Push your luck

Keep going for more, or stop and keep what you have.

**Shows it:** *Can't Stop*; blackjack

**Try it in two minutes:** Roll a die as often as you like and add it up; a 1 wipes your total. Stop whenever you want.

**Build it:** Each press adds a point and a chance of losing everything; a second pad banks.
