# Feedback ("juice")

Every action gets a sound, light, or shake that makes it feel good.

**Shows it:** *Peggle*

**Try it in two minutes:** Play rock-paper-scissors where the room cheers every win. Then play it in silence.

**Build it:** Every cue also fires a light and a sound on `/stage`; take it away and play again.
