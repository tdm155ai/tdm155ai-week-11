# Cooperation

Everyone wins or loses together.

**Shows it:** *Pandemic*

**Try it in two minutes:** The group counts to twenty; anyone may say the next number, but two voices at once means starting over.

**Build it:** A shared bar on `/stage` that rises only when every player hits their pad in the same window.
