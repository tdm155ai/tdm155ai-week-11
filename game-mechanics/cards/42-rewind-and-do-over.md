Go back and play the last moment again, changed.

**Shows it:** *Braid*; *Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time*

**Try it in two minutes:** Rewind the last ten seconds of a scene and play them again, changing one thing.

**Build it:** A recorded cue history on `/launch` that can be replayed from any point.
