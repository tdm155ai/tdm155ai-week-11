# The spect-actor

Watchers can step in and change the play.

**Shows it:** *Twitch Plays Pokémon*

**Try it in two minutes:** Anyone watching can say "stop", step in, and replace a player.

**Build it:** Audience phones or voices send cues that the player on stage must take.
