# Debt and obligation

You owe something and cannot leave until it is paid.

**Shows it:** *Monopoly* (rent and mortgages)

**Try it in two minutes:** You owe the room one laugh. You can't sit down until you get it.

**Build it:** `/voice` listens for a word or a laugh; until it hears it, the exit cue stays locked.
