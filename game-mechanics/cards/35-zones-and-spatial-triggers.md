# Zones and spatial triggers

Stepping into a part of the room makes something happen.

**Shows it:** Red Light, Green Light

**Try it in two minutes:** Tape three zones on the floor; each one makes a different sound when someone stands in it.

**Build it:** `/track/zones`: a column lights when occupied; moving on red costs a life.
