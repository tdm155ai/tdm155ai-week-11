# Roles and role swap

Each player has a different power, and the roles can change hands.

**Shows it:** *Pandemic* (role cards)

**Try it in two minutes:** Play a short scene; halfway through, the highest-status character becomes the lowest.

**Build it:** Roles dealt as cards; a cue on `/launch` reshuffles them mid-game.
