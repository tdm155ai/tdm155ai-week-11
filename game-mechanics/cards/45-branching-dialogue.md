# Branching dialogue

The player picks what to say, and the conversation forks.

**Shows it:** *Mass Effect*; Choose Your Own Adventure books

**Try it in two minutes:** Write a conversation on colored cards: one opener, three replies, and what each reply leads to.

**Build it:** Colored dialogue cards under `/draw-a-box`; the projector plays the chosen branch.
