# Goal and win condition

The state that ends the game and says who won.

**Shows it:** Chess (checkmate)

**Try it in two minutes:** Play tic-tac-toe, then change the goal: whoever makes three in a row loses.

**Build it:** A `/stage` page that watches one number and fires an end cue when it is reached.
