# Incremental growth

Numbers grow by themselves, and the player tunes the rate.

**Shows it:** *Cookie Clicker*; *Universal Paperclips*

**Try it in two minutes:** Every minute, each player's pile of paper clips doubles, unless they spend some to change the rule.

**Build it:** Claude Code builds a one-page clicker on any theme: three resources, each one feeding the next.
