*Wordle* is a small game: a five-letter word, six guesses, and a colour on every letter. Small enough that the rules for our version fit in one file.

**You need:** a laptop with Claude Code, this repository running on your machine (see its README: `cd nextjs`, `pnpm install`, `pnpm dev -p 3000`), and a partner to play your version.

## Do

1. **Play it.** Play a round of [our Wordle](/games/wordle).
2. **Open the file that holds its rules:** `nextjs/lib/games/wordle.js`. Ask Claude Code to explain how it works before you change anything.
3. **Decide on your remix.** Change one thing, in your own way: the length of the word, the number of guesses, the words it picks from, how a guess is scored, what the colours mean, what counts as winning. Or remix another daily word game you know, like *Connections* or *Spelling Bee*, starting from what you've learned here.
4. **Make the change with Claude Code.** Say which mechanic you are changing, and why.
5. **Play it again,** then hand it to your partner. Watch them play without explaining it.

## Bring back

- What you changed, in one sentence, and which mechanic it touches.
- What your partner did that you didn't expect.
- More ideas to try are on [Building games](/games).
