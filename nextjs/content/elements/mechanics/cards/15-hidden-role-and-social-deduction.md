**Shows it:** *Werewolf* (Mafia); *Among Us*

**Try it in two minutes:** Deal one secret card to each player; one card says "traitor". Talk for three minutes, then vote.

**Build it:** A bot sends each player a role by direct message; the room votes on `/stage`.
