Jesse Schell's *The Art of Game Design: A Book of Lenses* (2008) breaks every game into four elements: **mechanics** (the rules and what they let you do), **story** (the events that unfold), **aesthetics** (how it looks, sounds, and feels), and **technology** (what it is made with, from dice to a phone). He calls them the elemental tetrad. Change one and the other three move.

- **[The tetrad](tetrad.md):** the four elements and how they lean on each other.
- **[Mechanics](mechanics/README.md):** all 50 on one list, by family, each with a game that shows it.
- **[Technology](technology.md):** what makes a mechanic possible, including the NFC cards in this repo.
