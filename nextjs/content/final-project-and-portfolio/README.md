**What you'll hand in:**

- **A final project:** a new piece of media, a level or two up from your first project.
- **A portfolio** of everything you did this term, the final project included.
- **One final oral reflection:** a recorded walkthrough of your portfolio, your final project, and where the project sits within the portfolio.

## The dates

- **Wednesday, December 9: the rough draft.** A rough draft of your final project and your portfolio is due. That day there's an optional work-in-progress gallery, with food, during our regular meeting time or at a time we'll announce.
- **Monday, December 14, by 5:00 p.m.: the final.** The final project, the portfolio, and the final oral reflection are due, with the external drive that holds all your media. The drive is a physical thing, so it has to be in our hands by 5:00.

## The final project

A piece of media **a level or two up from your first project**: if that was one video, one podcast, or a simple website, make something more complex, or more complex in how it uses AI. For example:

- **a game**, or one well-made game mechanic in something that isn't a game;
- **a real-time tool for live shows**, something that augments a live performance;
- **a media installation** or another interactive piece;
- **a set of tools** for making more complex videos or podcasts, or many more;
- **a larger-scale piece of media**, or a **web-based multimodal essay or story**;
- **a system of agents** that is your creative project or makes your projects for you.

You can revise your first project and build on it, and additional projects are welcome, as many as you like. **Work together, hand in alone:** collaborate and help each other, but each of you submits your own project and portfolio, naming each collaborator and their part.

## The portfolio

Your final project and **all the work you did this term**: your first project, every practice, and your weekly videos, as links at least (nothing has to be public on the internet). Don't just list it: **use Claude Code to analyze, synthesize, and augment your work** in original and insightful ways: the threads that run through it, the moves you kept making, and what changed between September and December.

Above all, show us **what you did, with or without AI, that is special**: what you're proud of, and what you'll take with you to whatever comes next. Reflect on AI and media production, and on how they relate to knowledge work, learning, higher education, industry, or startups. If you revised earlier work, say so and point to what changed. We'll work on portfolios together in our last class, including how to compress videos so they're small enough to share.

## The final oral reflection

Record yourself **walking through your portfolio and your final project**, out loud, and talk about where the final project sits among everything else you made. It can be as long as you like and doesn't need to be polished: improvise your way through the material, as if showing it to someone beside you. Afterward, Claude can find the best sound bites in it; we'll give you a skill for that.

- **Where:** as a Zoom call or any other screen recording, or **in the Learning Lab**: you're more than welcome to come in and record in our space any time between our last class (December 9) and December 14.
- **When:** as late as December 14, as you hand everything in. Recording it the week before is easier if you want to use it in the portfolio.

## What to hand in

- [ ] **A GitHub repository (or a folder) with all your work in it**, private if you like, shared with us: the final project, the first project, every practice, the weekly videos (at least as links), the portfolio, and the final oral reflection.
- [ ] **Video links that stay stable**, so we can find and download every video. Nothing has to be public; we just need to get to it.
- [ ] **Unique identifiers in your file names**, so we know we have exactly the files you mean.
- [ ] **The external drive** we give you, with all your media on it (we already have what you've shared so far), in our hands by **5:00 p.m. on Monday, December 14**. We keep it safe from then on.
