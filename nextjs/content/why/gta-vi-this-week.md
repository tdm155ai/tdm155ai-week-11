*Grand Theft Auto VI* comes out on **Thursday, November 19, 2026**, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, unlocking at midnight local time around the world, starting in New Zealand ([OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/15398/grand-theft-auto-vi); [Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026)). Reviews came out on November 18. This page collects them as a live case for [Trash to canon](/why/trash-to-canon): the series that was once the leading example in arguments about violent games is now reviewed as art, as an epic, and as a love story.

## The New York Times: a Critic's Pick

- **Review:** Yussef Cole, November 18, 2026, [nytimes.com](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/18/arts/grand-theft-auto-vi-review.html). The Times does not score games; this one is a Critic's Pick.
- **The line the Times chose to share:** "This is a game about love and relationships, their joy and heartache, and the work required to make them successful." *(As quoted by [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/grand-theft-auto-6-review-roundup-rockstars-magnum-opus-a-great-love-story-and-a-new-bar-for-open-worlds/).)*
- **PC Gamer on the fact of the review:** "You know a videogame has broken containment when the grey lady is getting in on the review beat." (Joshua Wolens, November 18)

## The scores

- **Metacritic:** 99, from more than 150 critic reviews, 100% positive on PS5, which coverage says ties *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* for the site's highest score ([Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/)).
- **OpenCritic:** 99 average, 100% of critics recommend it ([OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/15398/grand-theft-auto-vi)).

## The panic, now told as history

- **The UK government:** the Culture Secretary, Lisa Nandy, called the game "something that we can be really proud of." PC Gamer's Andy Chalk: "Grand Theft Auto was once the poster child for everything insidious, dangerous, and downright wrong about videogames, but now we have a member of the UK government—a secretary of state, no less—talking about how the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6 is something the whole country should be proud of." ([PC Gamer, November 13](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/22-years-after-the-hot-coffee-controversy-uk-politician-says-grand-theft-auto-6-is-something-that-we-can-be-really-proud-of/))
- **The Guardian:** "Grand Theft Auto has forever been a lightning rod for moral panic over video game violence." And: "few would seriously venture that Breaking Bad endorses cooking meth – but when it comes to video games, where the player takes an active role, they are often seen to be lionising the behaviour they depict." ([Keza MacDonald, November 15](https://www.theguardian.com/games/ng-interactive/2026/nov/15/grand-theft-auto-vi-inside-rockstar-games))
- **Polygon:** "It's no wonder that, after decades of moral panic, GTA once again earns its title as public enemy #1 — only this time, Rockstar accomplishes that less through bloodshed and more with an empathetic love story built for an era of stark economic uncertainty."

This week's coverage mentions the panic only as history.

## Dickens, novels, film, TV

- **Dickens:** a week before launch the Guardian placed the series beside "those journalistic 19th-century novels by the likes of Zola and Thackeray," *Middlemarch*, and "Dickens opening Bleak House with that unimprovable one-word sentence: "London."" The same piece quotes the critic Christopher Byrd calling GTA "a totalising artwork." ([Christian Donlan, November 9](https://www.theguardian.com/games/2026/nov/09/grand-theft-auto-creators-gta))
- **Film:** NPR: "It goes from Badlands to Die Hard with the speed of a muscle car ... From True Romance to Heat like that's just the natural arc of any American love story."
- **Prestige TV:** Polygon and IGN both describe the story as running like a multi-season prestige TV show.

## The dissent

Not everyone agrees it is art yet. Digitally Downloaded: "However, as a likely de facto representative of video games, I have grave issues with GTA VI as a work of art." Its argument is that the game "performs the gestures of satire without following through," unlike Nabokov's *Lolita*, which gives readers the tools to see through its narrator ([Digitally Downloaded, November 18](https://www.digitallydownloaded.net/2026/11/review-grand-theft-auto-vi-sony-playstation-5.html)). Ars Technica found the satire's jokes about "wokeness" stale ([Kyle Orland](https://arstechnica.com/gaming/2026/11/grand-theft-auto-vi-the-ars-technica-review/)).

## The money and the people who made it

- **Pre-orders:** Sensor Tower estimates more than $1.65 billion in pre-order sales before launch, "an unprecedented amount for a game" ([Yahoo Finance, November 18](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/grand-theft-auto-vi-could-be-the-biggest-entertainment-launch-in-history-123431121.html)). These are estimates, not Rockstar's figures.
- **Launch day:** the Washington Post reports time-off requests for Thursday running about 20% above normal, and Logitech giving its employees the day off ([via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/music/articles/grand-theft-auto-vi-cultural-100000808.html)).
- **Labor:** Rockstar's UK workers are voting from November 12 to 26 on recognizing a union ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/software/2026/11/12/rockstar-workers-vote-on-union-recognition-as-gta-vi-launch-looms/5309319)), and closing arguments in a tribunal over the firing of 34 workers in 2025 were heard on launch eve ([Cartoon Brew](https://cartoonbrew.com/artist-rights/rockstar-union-busting-claims-gta-vi-launch-269528.html)).

## Questions

- The Times reviewed a game in its arts pages and chose to share a sentence about love. What does that choice say about the paper's audience, and about the game?
- Most of the praise compares GTA VI to older forms: the novel, the epic, the crime film, prestige TV. Is that how a form becomes canon, by being compared to the last one?
- What does the game do that none of those forms can? Look at the [mechanics](/elements/mechanics).
